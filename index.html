About Us

Founded in July, our organization is dedicated to training newly formed and inexperienced Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) teams, empowering them to rise up and compete in the competitive scene. We bridge the gap between amateur play and competitive glory by providing development resources and exciting opportunities for new rosters to build their reputation—highlighted by our flagship, recurring league, Heroes of Olympus.

Mission

01

To scout, mentor, and elevate grassroots esports talent by providing structured competition, skill development, and a platform for emerging teams to prove themselves.

Vision

02

To become a premier multi-title esports hub recognized for fostering competitive excellence, fairness, and opportunity across the amateur gaming ecosystem.

Roadmap & Milestones

Short-Term

Build a 500+ Member Community

Expanding our Community and player base to create an active ecosystem of teams, scrim partners, and passionate spectators.

Long-Term

Multi-Title Expansion

Scaling our league infrastructure beyond MLBB into major titles including Call of Duty: Mobile (CODM), Valorant, and beyond.
